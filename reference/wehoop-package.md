# wehoop: Access Women's Basketball Play by Play Data

A utility for working with women's basketball data. A scraping and
aggregating interface for the 'WNBA Stats' API <https://stats.wnba.com/>
of the Women's National Basketball Association ('WNBA'), the 'ESPN'
women's college basketball and 'WNBA' statistics, National Collegiate
Athletic Association ('NCAA') women's basketball statistics, 'Fox
Sports', 'Basketball-Reference', 'Her Hoop Stats', 'Barttorvik' and the
'WNBA' referee assignments published by the National Basketball
Association ('NBA') officiating site. It also reads international
women's basketball: International Basketball Federation ('FIBA')
competitions, the Spanish Basketball Federation ('FEB') leagues,
Germany's women's Basketball Bundesliga ('DBBL'), Japan's 'W League',
Australia's Women's National Basketball League ('WNBL'), the Turkish
Basketball Federation leagues and leagues served by 'Genius Sports' and
'Sportradar Atrium'. Loaders read pre-scraped play-by-play, box score,
schedule and model datasets from the 'SportsDataverse' data releases. It
provides users with the capability to access the game play-by-plays, box
scores, standings and results to analyze the data for themselves.
