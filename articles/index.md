# Articles

### All vignettes

- [ESPN basketball endpoints -- WBB &
  WNBA](https://wehoop.sportsdataverse.org/articles/espn-endpoints.md):

  A tour of the ESPN basketball endpoint wrappers in `wehoop` – around
  180 of them – grouped by use case: game data, team detail, athlete
  coverage, per-event enrichments, league-wide catalogs, WNBA
  draft/transactions/free-agency endpoints, and the post-3.0.0 core-v2
  deep expansion (athlete career, team-season stats, per-game player
  box, play personnel, typed-detail companions).

- [Getting Started with wehoop: WNBA and Women's College Basketball Data
  in
  R](https://wehoop.sportsdataverse.org/articles/getting-started-wehoop.md):

  Install wehoop, learn which function family reads which source, load
  WNBA box scores and play-by-play, and make your first women’s
  basketball chart in R.

- [WNBA Stats API Parameter
  Descriptions](https://wehoop.sportsdataverse.org/articles/parameter-descriptions.md):

  A searchable reference table of every query parameter used by the WNBA
  Stats API endpoints wrapped in `wehoop` – parameter name, description,
  accepted values, and which functions accept it.

- [Women's College Basketball
  Cookbook](https://wehoop.sportsdataverse.org/articles/wbb-cookbook.md):

  A storytelling tour of women’s college basketball analysis with
  `wehoop` – recipes from a single game to a full bracket, plus NCAA NET
  rankings, and a running commentary on how to read the package’s
  function names.

- [WNBA
  Cookbook](https://wehoop.sportsdataverse.org/articles/wnba-cookbook.md):

  A storytelling tour of WNBA analysis with `wehoop` – recipes from a
  single box score to a full season, with a running commentary on how
  the package’s function names are built so you can guess the next one.

- [WNBA Player Impact Model: RAPM, SPM, BPM, WAR and DARKO-Style
  Projections](https://wehoop.sportsdataverse.org/articles/wnba-player-impact-model.md):

  How the WNBA player impact ratings behind load_wnba_player_impact()
  are built: possession RAPM, an SPM prior, box plus/minus, WAR and a
  DARKO-style projection.

- [WNBA and Women's College Basketball Shot Charts in
  R](https://wehoop.sportsdataverse.org/articles/wnba-womens-college-basketball-shot-charts.md):

  Draw WNBA and NCAA women’s basketball shot charts in R with wehoop and
  sportyR: makes and misses, hexbin density, shot zones and FG% by zone.

- [Women's Basketball Advanced Metrics in R: Possessions, Pace, Ratings
  and the Four
  Factors](https://wehoop.sportsdataverse.org/articles/womens-basketball-advanced-metrics.md):

  Compute WNBA and women’s college basketball advanced stats in R from
  wehoop box scores: possessions, pace, ratings, four factors, TS% and
  usage.

- [Women's College Basketball Player Value: Box Plus-Minus
  Model](https://wehoop.sportsdataverse.org/articles/womens-college-basketball-player-value-model.md):

  How the NCAA women’s basketball box plus-minus ratings behind
  load_wbb_player_value() are built, how stable they are season to
  season, and how to use them.

- [Women's College Basketball RAPM: League-Wide and Within-Team Player
  Impact](https://wehoop.sportsdataverse.org/articles/womens-college-basketball-rapm-model.md):

  How wehoop’s NCAA women’s basketball RAPM ratings are built from
  possessions and lineups, how to read them, and how to fit WNBA RAPM
  with wnba_rapm().

- [Women's College Basketball Team Ratings: Opponent-Adjusted
  Efficiency](https://wehoop.sportsdataverse.org/articles/womens-college-basketball-team-ratings-model.md):

  How the opponent-adjusted NCAA women’s basketball team ratings behind
  load_wbb_ratings() work: adjusted offense, defense, tempo and net
  rating, with checks.

- [Women's College Basketball Win Probability
  Model](https://wehoop.sportsdataverse.org/articles/womens-college-basketball-win-probability-model.md):

  How the in-game win probability columns in wehoop’s NCAA women’s
  basketball play-by-play are built, and how well calibrated they are on
  a full season.

- [Women's International Basketball Data in R: FIBA, EuroLeague Women,
  WNBL, W League and
  More](https://wehoop.sportsdataverse.org/articles/womens-international-basketball-leagues.md):

  A guide to wehoop’s international women’s basketball functions: FIBA
  World Cup, Olympics, EuroLeague Women, WNBL, W League, LFB, WBBL, DBBL
  and KBSL.
